Each scene object has:
- id: the exact supplied shot id.
- representation: "illustration".
- narration_excerpt: an exact nonempty substring of the supplied shot's
  narration_excerpt. Preserve its qualifiers and timing. Full scene_text helps

  how the object/camera movement reveals it. Production metadata only.

  end_target:[x,y,z], fov:40}. Positions within -30..30, targets within -12..12,

  kind is "chip", "rack", "satellite", "robot", "data", or "object".
  These are recognizable local mesh assemblies: chip has board/package/pins,
  rack has stacked server bays, satellite has a body and solar wings, robot has
  a head/torso/arms/legs, and data depicts a physical signal object.
  For unfamiliar subjects, "object" may specify primitive "box", "sphere",
  "cylinder", or "torus". Prefer a recognizable assembly when appropriate.
  position/end_position: [x,y,z], coordinates -12..12. Keep a small uncluttered

  color: six-digit #RRGGBB.
  explode/end_explode: 0..1, ONLY for chip/rack/satellite, to separate and then
  inspect meaningful assembly layers. Do not claim that invented layers are a
  real product's actual architecture.
  motion_start/motion_end: normalized shot progress 0..1 with start<end,
  normally .12 and .72. Hold the final spatial relationship for reading.
  action: "reveal", "translate", "rotate", "explode", or "hold".

  supplied relationship_excerpt is nonempty and actually supports that link.
  Without a grounded relationship excerpt, omit links entirely.
